I'm Arindam, a software engineer. I work mostly on the backend, and these
days a lot of that is on the AI side: gateways and the infrastructure around
models. I'm curious about how things actually work, and I like doing deep
dives until I understand them. This site is where those end up.

## What I write about

- **Running LLMs in production.** Model servers, health checks, failure
  modes, and the fixes that came out of them.
- **Backend engineering and system design.** The pieces around the model
  that decide whether a system stays up.
- **Things I had to understand properly.** If I needed to draw it to get it,
  there's probably a diagram.

## How this site works

There are two sections. The [[blog/|blog]] is hand-written. The
[[study/|study section]] is a knowledge base of topic notes, and a page only
lands there after I've read and checked that exact version.

The site is built with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/).
