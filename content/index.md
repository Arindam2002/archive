Hi, I'm Arindam. This is where I keep the two kinds of writing I actually go
back to: notes I made while learning something, and posts I sat down and
wrote on purpose.

## Study

The [[study/|study section]] is a knowledge base. Each page is one topic I
worked through properly, with the links between topics, the flashcards I use
to test myself, and a diagram where a picture beats a paragraph. Pages land
here only after I have read that exact version, so nothing shows up that I
have not gone through myself. Follow the links or open the graph and wander.

## Blog

The [[blog/|blog section]] is hand-written. Longer, more opinionated, and
slower to appear. When a post leans on something from the study section it
links straight to it, so you can go one level deeper without me repeating
myself.
