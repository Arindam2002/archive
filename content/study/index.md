Interview prep topics, one page each: backend engineering, system design,
data structures and algorithms, and LLM infrastructure. Every topic has an
overview diagram, a flashcard deck at the end, and links to the topics it
builds on, so the graph view shows how the material connects.

These pages are exported from a private study base once a topic has been
read and approved. Nothing here is auto-published: a topic that changes
after approval is held back until it is reviewed again. New topics appear as
they clear that bar.
